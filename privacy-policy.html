Summary

FitTrack Daily is a personal diet, exercise and reminder tracking app. Your data stays on your device. We do not run a server that receives your personal or health data, we do not sell data, and the app contains no advertising and no analytics trackers.

Data you enter in the app

The app lets you enter and track:

  • Profile details you choose to provide (such as name, age, weight, height and goals)
  • Meals, calories, exercises, workouts, water intake and custom reminders
  • App settings (reminder times, sounds, preferences)

All of this information is stored only in the app's local database on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any server we operate, and we have no access to it.

Data processed by Google services

The app uses Firebase App Check (a Google service) purely to verify that requests come from a genuine copy of the app (app integrity / abuse prevention). For this purpose Google may process limited technical information such as device and app integrity signals, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not use Firebase Analytics, advertising IDs, or crash-reporting services that collect personal data.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Notifications — to show the meal, workout and water reminders you schedule.
  • Exact alarms — so the reminders you set fire at the exact time you chose.
  • Run at startup (boot completed) — to restore your scheduled reminders after the device restarts.
  • Internet / network state — used by Firebase App Check for app-integrity verification.
  • Vibrate / wake lock — so a reminder alert can wake the device and vibrate, as expected for an alarm-style reminder.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or health information with third parties. Your tracking data never leaves your device.

Data deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it completely:

  • You can clear your progress history and reset your plan inside the app's Settings at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all app data from your device.

Children

FitTrack Daily is intended for users aged 18 and above and is not directed at children under 13.

Health disclaimer

FitTrack Daily is a personal tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified health professional for medical guidance.
